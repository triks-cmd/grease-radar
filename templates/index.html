Methodology

Risk Score Calculation

35% Frequency number of incidents
30% Recurrence distinct incident months
20% Recency how recently incidents occurred
15% Spatial density nearby incident cells

These weights are baseline heuristics for validation, not scientifically validated constants. Risk scores are heuristic rankings, not calibrated probabilities of future blockages.

Backtesting Methodology

Historical evaluation uses a 70-30 temporal split. Hotspot scores are calculated using only data available before the evaluation period to prevent information leakage. The system reports the percentage of future incidents that occurred in cells identified as high-risk.

Data Sources

This prototype uses synthetic demonstration data. For production, integrate official Vilniaus vandenys incident data. The system accepts CSV files with latitude, longitude, and date columns.

Limitations

  • Risk scores are heuristic rankings, not calibrated probabilities
  • Spatial proximity does not prove causation
  • System does not identify specific businesses as responsible
  • Fuel calculations are scenario-based estimates, not measured production
  • Backtesting on demo data does not indicate real-world performance
